Security & AI

Controls are part of the product.

Access, privacy and audit are designed in from the start, not added on top.

Role-based access

Roles, permissions and approvals decide what each person sees and does.

Customer separation

One customer, property or owner group can never reach another's data.

Financial-data firewall

Platform operator staff do not see customers' financial numbers.

Tamper-evident audit

Append-only audit history and logged operator access.

Encryption

Encrypted in transit and at rest, with tenant-scoped keys.

Safe uploads

Every file is type-checked and scanned before it can be opened.

Sessions and devices

Idle timeouts, remote sign-out, shared-device PIN and kiosk mode.

Backups and recovery

Continuous backups including an immutable copy, with restore tests.
